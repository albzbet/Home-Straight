# homestr8 brand brief

Paste this into a new chat when making videos, graphics or other material for homestr8. Attach the kelpie logo
(`assets/logo/kelpie.png`, white on see-through) and the mockup (`design/homestr8-mockup-copper.webp`) with it.

## The app in a nutshell

homestr8 is an Australian app that helps adults take control of their gambling. It's an 8-week morning program, about
10 minutes a day (5 on a busy day), built on CBT (cognitive behavioural therapy). Each session has a short video, a guided
audio, a quick check-in and one small thing to try that day. Around it: Urge SOS (breathing and tools for when an urge hits),
progress and money kept, weekly missions, a savings goal, if-then plans, "Your loop" (each person maps their own gambling
cycle and where to break it), BetStop sign-up for people who choose to stop completely, and Bookie Tricks Exposed (short
episodes on how the gambling industry keeps people hooked).

- **Who it's for:** mainly Australian blokes, 18 to 35. Tradies, office workers, footy fans. Mostly sports betting on the
  phone; some pokies.
- **Tagline:** LESS NOISE. MORE LIFE.
- **Positioning:** a self-help program, not medical treatment. It complements Gambling Help Online (1800 858 858, free, 24/7),
  never replaces it.

## Tone

Like a good mate who's been there: straight-talking, warm, dry Australian humour, no BS. Never preachy, never clinical,
never shaming. "Stop completely" is recommended, but it's their call. No promises like "you'll quit for good". Plain words,
short sentences. Turn the blame on the betting companies and the way the products are designed, not on the person.

Brand words: bold, modern, masculine, confident, supportive, real, Australian, no BS, premium.

## Colours

Dark only. Charcoal background, copper for the main action and highlights, white for text.

## Type

- **Headings:** Bebas Neue, always in capitals. Often the last word or phrase in copper, e.g. "BOOKIE TRICKS **EXPOSED**",
  "URGE **SOS**", "SMALL CHOICES. **BIG FREEDOM.**"
- **Everything else:** Inter (regular, medium, semibold, bold).
- Both are free Google Fonts.

## Logo

- **The kelpie:** a line-drawn kelpie's head in side profile, white on a see-through background. It's drawn in white on dark
  backgrounds, charcoal on copper.
- **Wordmark:** the kelpie next to HOMESTR8 in Bebas Neue, white, with the **8 in copper**. Under it: LESS NOISE. MORE LIFE.
  (Inter, spaced out capitals).
- **App icon:** charcoal kelpie on a copper square.
- Give the kelpie room to breathe; don't stretch it, add effects or change its shape.

## Imagery

- Warm, golden-hour Australian scenes: coastal headlands at sunrise or sunset, beaches, footy ovals, utes, backyards,
  building sites. A bloke and his kelpie is the signature image.
- Real, ordinary blokes, not models. Mates, partners, kids, dogs, being outdoors, doing things.
- Warm copper and sunset tones over dark, moody shadows.
- **Never:** betting screens or apps, bet slips, odds, pokies machines, cards, chips, dice, racing or horses, cash
  spilling, trophies, slot-style lights. Gambling is never made to look fun or glamorous. If a scene involves betting, show
  the person's face lit by the phone, never the screen. A gaming room is a glow through a doorway, never machines up close.
- No shame imagery (head in hands is fine once, but not as the brand's look).

## The look of the app (for anything that shows it)

Dark charcoal screens, stone cards with soft rounded corners, a copper main button, Bebas Neue headlines with a copper
last word, green progress bars, simple line icons. Every screen has an "Urge SOS" button (copper outline) and a white
"Get help now" button at the top.

## The Wayno series (the daily videos)

A short daily motion-comic series that runs alongside the 8 weeks. Same look as above: charcoal, copper, warm sunset light.

- **Wayno**, 29, a sparky. Funny, generous, losing more to sports betting on his phone than anyone knows ($8,400 on a
  credit card his partner doesn't know about). Narrates in his own head.
- **Mel**, 28, his partner, a nurse. Warm, sharp, no fool.
- **Jono**, 31, his best mate since school. The funniest bloke in any room; plays the pokies after late shifts and hides it.
- **Tommo**, 30, runs the boys' group chat; always has a multi going. Loud, loyal, not a villain.
- **Dad (Kev)**, 58, a bet on the footy every weekend for forty years, and a "system". **Mum (Sue)**, 56.
- **Red**, Wayno's red kelpie (the logo kelpie in real life). Morning walks at sunrise.

Episodes: 45 to 60 seconds, 16:9 for the app (9:16 cuts for social). Bold captions in Bebas Neue (works with the sound
off). Each episode: a quick "previously", Wayno meets the day's lesson in real life, then a cliffhanger line in copper.
Voice: one Australian man, about 30, dry and warm, never preachy. Music low and driving.

The core idea underneath: gambling runs in a loop (trigger → thought → urge → bet → buzz → cost → back to the trigger),
and every link is a place you can break it. That's a skill, and skills can be learned.
